📲 Install App on Your Phone

Instead of downloading an APK, here is the easiest way to install this app directly on your phone exactly like a native app.

How to install (Takes 10 seconds):

  1. Open your phone's Camera and scan the QR code above.
  2. Wait 1 second, then tap the link to open the app in your phone's browser (like Chrome or Safari).
  3. Tap the 3 dots menu (⋮) in the top right corner of Chrome.
    (On iPhone/Safari, tap the 'Share' icon at the bottom).
  4. Select "Add to Home screen".
  5. Done! The app will now appear on your phone's home screen alongside your other apps.

Note: This is faster, safer, and does not require you to deal with updating an APK file later.
